# app — The screens

One job: the screens the person sees.

## What it owns

Layout, wording, the advice shown beside a result, and the screens of the first-run walk.

## What it never does

It never talks to the database or to Google, and it never calls a model.

Piece 1 first builds it. Its seam is written together with its code and tests in that piece. Until then the [target map](../../map/target.md) is the plan for it.
